# 10 — Pitch outline (12 slides)

Presentation skeleton — every slide maps to a doc in this repo so the deck and the build never drift apart.

1. **Cold open — the broken promise.** "Every habit app gives you XP. Name one thing XP ever bought you." Screenshot of a level-99 Habitica account next to an empty hand. *(01-vision)*
2. **How real games do it.** Fortnite pays in skins and V-Bucks. Progression only works when it pays out something you want. Habit apps copied the grind and forgot the loot.
3. **The idea.** Your own money, locked in a vault, unlocked by your own effort, spent on your own wishlist. Show the core-loop diagram. *(05-flows §1, §3)*
4. **Why it's safe to build.** The app never pays rewards — users fund themselves. Cheaters only cheat themselves. Zero payout fraud surface. *(03-business-rules R1, R13)*
5. **The frame flip.** Beeminder/stickK punish failure; we celebrate success. Positioning table. *(01-vision)*
6. **The product.** Pixel-art screens: Quest Board, Vault, Loot Shop. Live demo of mockup.html or embedded screenshots. *(08-design-direction, mockup)*
7. **Who pays and how.** Subscription backbone + float + interchange. "We never tax the loop" as the trust moat. Unit-economics table. *(04-monetization)*
8. **The rails already exist.** Brazil-first: Pix QR pay, BaaS (Celcoin/QI Tech), card issuing with real-time authorization (Pomelo/Dock) — the quest-gate enforced at any checkout on earth. *(06-architecture, 09-risks)*
9. **Phased de-risking.** Phase 1 pure game (no money UI) → Phase 2 vault + Pix → Phase 3 platform. Each phase has gates and kill criteria — show the roadmap. *(07-roadmap)*
10. **Market.** Brazilian gamers 18–35 (size the niche), habit-app market growth, Pix penetration. *(research to add)*
11. **Moat.** The two-balance commitment mechanic + the trust promise (R1–R3 as public constitution) + a brand gamers actually want on their phone.
12. **Ask & milestones.** What you need (funding/partners/team) mapped to Phase 1 exit gates.
