# 04 — Monetization

## Principle

Never tax the loop (rule R3). The deposit is the moment of maximum trust-fragility and the redemption is the celebration — charging at either end poisons the core emotion. Revenue comes from around the loop:

| Stream | Phase | Who pays | Mechanics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subscription | 1+ | The user (knowingly) | Freemium; the wallet is a premium feature |
| Float yield | 2+ | The market | Locked balances sit in safeguarded accounts earning ~CDI via the BaaS partner; high Brazilian rates make the vault itself a revenue engine |
| Card interchange | 3 | The merchant | Prepaid interchange (capped ~0.7% in Brazil) on every card redemption |
| Lifetime unlock | 1+ (optional) | The anti-subscription niche | High-priced one-time tier, à la Habitica/Forest |

Note the alignment: float grows when users commit more and longer (ambitious quests); interchange grows when users succeed and redeem. We earn on both sides of the loop without the user ever seeing a fee. "Your money is always fully yours" is simultaneously our ethics and our best marketing line.

## Unit economics sketch (Phase 2–3, per funded Hero/month)

| Line | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Subscription revenue | +R$19,90 |
| Float (avg vault R$150 × ~1%/month) | +R$1,50 |
| Interchange (R$80 card spend × 0.7%) | +R$0,56 |
| BaaS costs (account + KYC amortized + Pix in/out) | −R$3 to −R$6 |
| Infra + support allocation | −R$2 |
| **Contribution** | **≈ +R$14–17** |

Free users cost ≈ R$0,30–0,60/month (infra only) — acceptable as acquisition. A funded wallet is gated behind the subscription precisely so no wallet can be cash-flow negative.

## What we never charge (public promise)

- Deposit fees, withdrawal fees, redemption fees, conversion fees, inactivity fees.
- We never sell wishlist or behavioral data (rule R15).

## Pricing experiments queue (Phase 1)

1. R$14,90 vs R$19,90 vs R$24,90 Hero price test.
2. Wallet waitlist CTA for free users ("Notify me when the Vault opens") — measures deposit intent before building fintech.
3. Annual-first checkout vs monthly-first.
